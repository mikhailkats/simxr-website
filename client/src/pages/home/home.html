Why it scales differently

Physical teleoperation scales by buying another robot. We scale by connecting another person.

Every skill starts with a person showing it inside a physics simulation: human judgement, not purely synthetic data. Our operators work from home, in parallel copies of the scene, with instant resets, and collection never spends your robot’s hours.

Headset

A Quest 3 and a browser.

An operator opens simxr.app, picks a server and is inside the robot. No install, no lab, no rig.

Servers

The nearest region, automatically.

GPU servers run in the EU and the US. Each operator is offered the one with the shortest round trip.

Data

Only clean attempts count.

Every demonstration is validated by replay before it reaches training. Rejects are our cost.
