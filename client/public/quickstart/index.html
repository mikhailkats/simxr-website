Operator quick start

Teleoperate a humanoid robot in two minutes.

You'll remotely operate a humanoid robot (Unitree G1) inside a photorealistic simulated living room, streamed live to your headset from our US cloud server. No account, no app install, no downloads — everything runs in the headset's built-in browser.

Open simxr.app in your headset → works best on Meta Quest 3 / 3S
● Live right now

The scene you'll step into.

Right now the server is running “Bright Living Room” — a photorealistic 3D-Gaussian-Splat reconstruction of a real living room, with a Unitree G1 humanoid robot standing at the table. Your headset streams this scene live, and your own hand movements drive the robot's hands in real time.

1 · simxr.app — the Live card, tap Connect
2 · Inside VR — the table, the steering wheel, the basket
If you're sitting, stand up. The robot stands at the table, so stand up too — that puts your eyes and hands at the robot's level and makes everything reach naturally.

Your task

As soon as you're in, hand-tracking is already active — no setup. Then, calmly:

  1. Pick up the steering wheel from the table with your left hand.
  2. Pass it across to your right hand.
  3. Place it into the basket.
  4. Done cleanly? The scene resets to its starting state on its own — ready to try again.

Move slowly and smoothly — the first few reaches feel unfamiliar, and within a minute your hands and the robot's hands click into sync. There's nothing to break: take your time and get a feel for it.

What you need
Meta Quest 3 or 3S

The tested and recommended headset. Other devices are not verified yet.

Home Wi-Fi

A regular home connection, 5 GHz preferred. Office and hotel networks often block streaming.

5 minutes

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to install. Open a page, press Connect, you're in.

Steps

From headset to robot.

  1. Open the browser inside your headset Put on the headset and launch the built-in browser app.
  2. Go to simxr.app Type simxr.app in the address bar. You'll see the live scene catalog.
  3. Connect to the US server Find the card marked ● Live and tap Connect, then confirm Enter VR when the browser asks.
  4. Look around, then reach out You're standing in the room. Put the controllers down and use your bare hands — the robot's hands follow yours. Controllers work too, but hands are the magic.
  5. Try the task Complete the task for the live scene — for the current room, that's the steering-wheel handoff described below.
  6. Done? Just exit Take off the headset or exit VR. The scene resets itself for the next operator about a minute later.
Good to know
  • One operator at a time. If the card shows “In session”, someone else is connected — try again in a few minutes.
  • The server is in Oregon, US. From North America the experience is smooth; from Europe you may notice slight latency — still fine for a first look.
  • Sessions may be recorded as robot demonstration data — the robot's motions, not video of you. Ask us for the data agreement if you'd like the details.
If something looks wrong

Troubleshooting.

SymptomFix
Card shows “In session” The server is busy with another operator — retry in ~5 minutes.
Black screen after connecting Exit VR, wait 2 minutes (the scene may be restarting), then reconnect.
Connects but no image, on office or hotel Wi-Fi Corporate networks often block the video stream (UDP). Switch to a phone hotspot.
First connect after a long idle feels slow Give it ~30 seconds — the first handshake warms up the stream.
