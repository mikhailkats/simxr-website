# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Robotics researchers, Physical AI teams, simulation engineers, prospective partners, customers, investors, and operators evaluating whether remote consumer-VR teleoperation can produce useful robot-learning demonstrations.

## Product Purpose

Sim XR shows and provides infrastructure for collecting human demonstrations in robot simulation, validating the data, training policies, and measuring policy performance in closed-loop evaluation. Success means a technically informed reader can understand the workflow, inspect the evidence, and distinguish validated simulation results from planned 3DGS, sim-to-real, and real-robot work.

## Brand Personality

Precise, physical, and evidence-led. The voice is confident without overclaiming, technically literate without becoming an internal engineering log, and human-centered without presenting Sim XR as a VR game.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark “robotics future” visuals, neon grids, decorative sci-fi interfaces, and cinematic gradients that replace evidence.
- Startup language that makes broad capability claims without showing the task, demonstrations, evaluation, and measured result.
- Template-like metric cards, repeated numbered labels, excessive all-caps copy, and long explanatory overlays that compete with footage.
- NVIDIA-green imitation or another company’s identity used as the visual system for Sim XR.

## Design Principles

1. Show the robot and the evidence before explaining the claim.
2. Keep one legible idea on screen at a time.
3. Use the established Sim XR light identity, near-black ink, white space, and blue emphasis.
4. Make human input visible as part of the technical system.
5. Label simulation results and future work with exact boundaries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for deterministic text and controls. Keep essential meaning independent of color, provide captions for voice-over, retain a useful no-audio experience, respect reduced-motion preferences on interactive surfaces, and keep critical text inside mobile-safe and video-safe areas.
